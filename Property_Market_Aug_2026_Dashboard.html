💡Key Insights — August 2026

🏙️ Gosford Crosses $1M — A Policy Milestone
The LGA's own CBD hits $1,088,000, up +$108k in just two months and +14.5% YoY. The long-running Gosford city centre discount is narrowing — this is the most significant milestone in this month's data.
⭐ Wyongah: Fastest-Rising Suburb at +21.6%
Newly entering "Close" territory at $972,500 with units also up +30% YoY. An outer northern suburb accelerating rapidly — likely to cross $1M within 6–9 months at this pace.
🌏 Northern Corridor: Broad, Sustained Growth
Tuggerawong +21.4%, Gorokan +13.2%, Wyee +14%, The Entrance +14.8%, Woy Woy +13.4%, Norah Head +11.7% — all sub-$1.1M. The northern Lake Munmorah corridor is the Coast's growth engine for the second consecutive report.
📉 Luxury Tier: Sustained Correction
Killcare Heights -17.9%, Matcham -14.9%, Blue Bay -14.3%, Point Frederick -12.7%, Erina Heights -10.1%. The $1.5M+ tier is declining for the second consecutive month — this is now a trend, not noise.
🔴 Koolewong: Rapid -$160k Decline
Fell from $1,135,000 to $975,000 in two months (-14.1%), losing its $1M+ status. At -12.6% in a single month, this waterfront suburb is the most alarming single movement in the dataset.
🔜 Wadalba: $10k from the Threshold
At $990,000 with +10.6% annual momentum and consistent monthly growth, Wadalba is the suburb most likely to cross $1M in the next data release.
⚠️ Noraville Retreats — Thin Market Confirmed
After briefly crossing $1M in June at $1,031,000, Noraville has pulled back to $950,000. Its June crossing was driven by 1–2 higher-value sales; the underlying median remains sub-$1M.
🏘️ Woongarrah — Housing Opportunity
A modern growth suburb with +9.9% YoY, $800/week rents, and zero established unit stock. The strongest candidate for Council advocacy of medium-density infill development.
