💡Key Insights

🆕 Two Suburbs Join the Million-Dollar Club
Narara and Noraville crossed $1M for the first time. Noraville's +20.9% annual growth is the most significant milestone in this month's data.
🏙️ The Gosford Paradox
The LGA's own CBD sits at $980k with just 161 interested buyers — the lowest of any near-$1M suburb. The city centre discount persists despite proximity to the threshold.
🌏 Northern Corridor Emerging
Gorokan (+14.2%), Toukley (+11.7%), Noraville (+20.9%), Wyee (+28.7%), Charmhaven (+11.6%) — the Lake Munmorah northern corridor is the Coast's new growth engine.
📉 Luxury Market Cooling
Matcham (-8.4%), Wagstaffe (-8.5%), Point Frederick (-6.9%) and North Avoca (-10.3%) all declining. The $2M+ tier is uniformly soft this month.
🏘️ San Remo — Housing Opportunity
Sub-$1M at $807k, growing +8.3%, 700 active buyers, zero unit stock. Prime candidate for Council to encourage medium-density affordable housing development.
👁️ Wyoming — Watch List
2,044 interested buyers for a $953k suburb is extraordinary — more than most $1.5M+ markets. Watch for $1M crossing within 12 months if momentum holds.
