💡 Key Insights — Oct 2026

📉↓ Median softening
Median house price eased $15,250 from September's $1,145,500. YoY growth also moderated to +2.81% from +3.65%. October reflects a mild correction after two months of gains, though the market remains broadly above $1M median.
✅✓ 51 suburbs at or above $1M
Net gain of 2 suburbs from September's 49. Noraville crossed the threshold at exactly $1,000,000. Wadalba confirmed its September crossing at $1,010,000. West Gosford also jumped above $1M this month (+28.7% MoM) but warrants independent verification given the magnitude of the move.
⚠️! Gosford & West Gosford anomalies
Gosford shows +50.4% YoY and +17.7% MoM at $1,555,000. West Gosford surged +28.7% MoM to $1,010,099. These extraordinary movements — in suburbs with notably low visit counts (251 and 133 respectively) — may reflect property-mix changes within the median calculation or other data effects. Without sales count data this cannot be verified. Do not use to represent LGA-wide appreciation.
🏆★ Demand: Wamberal leads
Wamberal (3,454 visits/property) overtakes September's leader Terrigal (now 3,014). Both are coastal lifestyle suburbs above $1.6M. Bateau Bay (2,773) remains highest-engagement suburb in the accessible $1M–$1.3M band — sustained buyer engagement (listing visits proxy, not unique buyers) continues despite median softening.
